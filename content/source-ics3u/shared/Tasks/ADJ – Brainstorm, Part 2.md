## Introduction

Earlier in this course, you started to [[ADJ – Brainstorm, Part 1|consider apps that you like to use, and why]], as part of your *App Design Journal*.

Recall the four stages of design:

1. Brainstorm
2. Plan
3. Prototype
4. Evaluate

Recall, also, that design is an iterative process, so each step can be revisited as needed:
